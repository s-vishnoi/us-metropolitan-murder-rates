\section*{Data availability}

The processed data necessary to reproduce the analyses and figures reported in this study are available to editors and reviewers upon request and will be deposited in a public repository upon publication. The underlying data were obtained from the Federal Bureau of Investigation Uniform Crime Reporting Program, the Inter-university Consortium for Political and Social Research, IPUMS National Historical Geographic Information System, the US Census Bureau, CDC WONDER, and the US Bureau of Labor Statistics. Some original source files are not redistributed because they remain subject to the access and redistribution conditions of their respective providers. Dataset identifiers and instructions for obtaining the original files are provided with the analysis code.
